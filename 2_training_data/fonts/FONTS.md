fonts under the **SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1** (full text in
`OFL.txt`): they may be used, copied and redistributed, also inside other
software, as long as they are not sold by themselves. They are not modified.

for modified versions of the fonts.

More fonts can be added: put any `.ttf` or `.otf` file in this folder, or
use `--system-fonts` to also use the fonts installed on your computer
(those stay on your computer: the pictures made with them are yours, the
fonts are not copied anywhere).
